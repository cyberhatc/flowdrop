phase 7: mobile share targets (Android sheet + bridge)

From another app, "Send to FlowDrop..." now opens the app with the
shared files/text prefilled and a device picker:
- receive_sharing_intent 1.9.0 (new dep) + ShareBridge wiring initial
  media and the live share stream into the controller
- AndroidManifest: ACTION_SEND / ACTION_SEND_MULTIPLE intent filters
- AppState.pendingShare + ShareItem; controller.ingestShare converts
  media into SendFile/text entries (unreadable files are skipped with a
  banner instead of failing the whole share)
- ShareSheet UI: review items, pick a discovered peer, send immediately
- iOS share extension still requires an App Group target — documented
  in docs/ARCHITECTURE.md (honest limitation, not faked)
- tests: initial/live/empty share via the plugin's mock hooks
